★ Field Guide · Page 1

Learn in 60 Seconds

Each turn does three things. Three things only.

1

Draw a Card

Pull from the Critter deck. Your hand is hidden — keep it that way.

2

Play an Animal

Place one critter onto an Ocean. Match the Ocean's species symbol to score.

3

Score Synergy

Adjacent species combo — bait fish feed game fish, coral shelters everything.

End of turn? Pass the deck. Game ends when the END GAME card surfaces — highest combined ocean wins.

The Rules of the Reef

Ocean Attachment

Animals don't sit on the table — they attach to an Ocean board in one of four positions. Position affects who they're "adjacent" to.

2

Star Abilities ★

Many cards have a ★ Star Ability — a special move that fires once when the card lands. Play Again. Draw Three. Steal a Coral.

3

End-Game Scoring

When the END GAME card surfaces, the round finishes and every Ocean tallies independently.

Base Points + Synergy + Ocean Bonus = Ocean Score
4

Special Cards

END GAME
Shuffled into the bottom third of the deck. When it appears, finish the round and tally — no warning.
Star Abilities
Marked with ★. Fire once on play. Most are "play again" or "draw three" but a few rewrite the board.

★ Our Story

Our Story

Growing up near the Jersey Shore, my family and I spent countless days in and around the ocean. As I got older and moved away, I realized how much those memories shaped my love for the ocean and the life in and around it.

That passion is a big part of why I created Currents & Critters. I wanted to build something fun that also gives back — helping support ocean cleanup and conservation so future generations can experience clean, healthy, and abundant oceans the same way I did growing up.
