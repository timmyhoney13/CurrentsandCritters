How to Play

Learn in 60 Seconds

Easy to learn, deep to master. Build the best underwater ecosystem before the END GAME card appears.

On Your Turn — Choose ONE:

1
Draw 2 cards from any mix of the Draw Pile and the Pool (face-up discard board).
2
Play 1 card — place an Ocean, or attach an animal to an existing Ocean. Pay the cost by discarding cards to the Pool.
3
Move 1 animal — relocate an animal from one of your Oceans to another. The card must keep the same orientation (Up, Down, Left, or Right). This uses your whole turn.
✓
End of turn: discard down to 10 cards if needed. Play passes clockwise.
🌊

Oceans — Your Foundation

  • All animals must attach to an Ocean you control
  • Oceans are placed left to right and can't be moved
  • 4 attachment slots: Top (birds/baitfish), Bottom (lobster/crab/goby), Left & Right (tuna, sharks, rays)
  • Playing an Ocean flips 1 card from the deck into the Pool
⭐

Star Abilities

  • Some cards have an optional ★ ability
  • Activate by discarding a card with the same symbol when you play the card
  • You may still play the card without activating the ★
  • ★ abilities must be used immediately — cannot be saved
  • Free Animals from ★ abilities come from your hand only
🔄

★ Play Again

  • If a ★ says "Play Again," the extra play is a full new turn action
  • You can draw, play a card, or place an Ocean on the extra play
  • Extra plays still work during the final turns at end game
🎴

The Pool

  • All discarded cards go face-up here — players can draw from it
  • When the Pool hits 10 cards: move all to a face-down pile and reset
  • If an ability lets you take from the Pool, you can't take back both cards you just discarded — only one
🏁

End Game

  • END GAME is shuffled into the bottom 10 cards of the deck at setup
  • When revealed, each player takes one final turn — then score
  • Add all printed points and ability bonuses across all your Oceans
  • Highest score wins
🐠

Special Rules

  • Animal Movement: spend your whole turn to move 1 animal keeping the same orientation
  • Shooting the Moon: play all 4 Mandarin Gobies → +20 points
  • Surf's Up: say it when leaving the table to pause your turn with no penalty
  • Advanced (2-player): each player controls two separate hands

About

A Two-Brother Passion Project

Currents & Critters started the way most great ideas do — at a kitchen table with too many snacks and not enough good card games. We're two brothers who grew up obsessed with the ocean: fishing trips, nature documentaries, and way too many hours watching coral reef footage.

We wanted a game that felt like the ocean — complex ecosystems, surprising synergies, and the satisfaction of watching something you built come together. So we made one. What started as a hand-drawn prototype on index cards became a commercial tabletop game.

Every card in the deck is a real species. Every mechanic ties back to how marine ecosystems actually work — predators, baitfish, coral foundations, and the birds that hunt from above. We care about getting the details right.

Our Ocean Mission

A portion of every game sold goes toward ocean conservation. We believe the best way to protect something is to make people fall in love with it first. Currents & Critters is our way of doing that — one card game at a time.
