Critter Coins, bonus XP, profile frames, and player icons are cosmetic/progression rewards only and do not affect gameplay. Critter Coins are the online game's cosmetic currency and are credited to your game account automatically after checkout. Every cosmetic you own can be given away: avatars, skins and backgrounds all trade to another account, along with Critter Coins, Season Pass vouchers and XP. Supporter Reef Wall placement is based on contribution amount, with the highest supporters featured first. 2% goes to ocean conservation.
