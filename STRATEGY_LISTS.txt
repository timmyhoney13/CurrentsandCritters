WHAT GOES WITH ALL THE STRATEGIES
==============================================================================

253 deck entries, 62 different cards, 14 plans a bot can commit to.
Generated by strategy_lists.py from the engine's own tables. Do not hand-edit.

HOW TO READ A PLAN
  pays for it   the multiplier or count chart the plan scores through. Collect
                the family without one of these and you have collected nothing.
  cards         every card whose points belong to this plan.
  goes with     the anchor pairs: cards the engine floors as going together,
                because one of them says so in print.
  same ocean    pairs that have to land on the SAME ocean, not just the same
                board. Only four effects in the deck actually read the ocean.
  at            table sizes and skill tiers the plan is offered at.
