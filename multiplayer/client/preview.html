📖 Quick Tutorial

🌊 Goal

Build the best ocean ecosystem and score the most points. The game ends when an END GAME card is played — each other player gets one final turn, then scores are tallied.

🃏 Your Turn

On your turn: draw 2 cards from the deck or pool, play an Ocean card to start a new ecosystem, or attach Animal cards to an existing ocean in the matching direction (Up / Down / Left / Right).

◆ Symbols & Payment

Every card has a symbol (Triangle, Square, Heart, Circle, Diamond). To activate a card's ability you must pay its cost by discarding cards from your hand. If the card requires a symbol match, at least one payment card must share that symbol — the game will block you if you try to play without it.

★ Star Abilities

Cards with ★ have a bonus Star Ability. Enable "★ Star ability" in the action bar, then play the card normally. One matching-symbol card from your hand is automatically used as the star payment.

🏆 Scoring

Cards score points based on your board: flat bonuses (+X), per-type bonuses (+X per Bird, etc.), and threshold bonuses (e.g. 1=5pts, 2=15pts, 3=30pts). Click the Score button in the notice bar during the game.

👥 Multiplayer Layout

With 2 players your opponent appears across the table (left). With 3 players the third player appears across the top. With 4 players the fourth appears on the right. Watch opponents to know when to trigger the END GAME card!
