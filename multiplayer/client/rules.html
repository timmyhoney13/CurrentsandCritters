Match Setup

  • Create a room with your host key and choose total players.
  • Each human claims a seat before the game can start.
  • Host starts the game once all required human seats are filled.

Turn Basics

  • On your turn, choose one legal action shown by the game UI.
  • You can draw, play an ocean, play an animal, or discard when required.
  • Some cards allow star actions like play-again, free plays, or extra draws.

Board Placement

  • Ocean cards create anchors for lanes and attached cards.
  • Direction-based cards are played to matching sides when legal.
  • The game enforces legal placement, costs, and symbol requirements.

Scoring and End

  • Score is calculated from card text, species synergies, and ocean effects.
  • When end-game triggers, final turns resolve and winner is announced.
  • Use restart to begin a new round in the same room.
