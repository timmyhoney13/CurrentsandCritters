About The Project

Tidal Wave is built to make online ocean-card matches feel smooth and reliable. The core focus is stable multiplayer flow, reconnect confidence, and clean game-state consistency during real sessions.

What It Is

A server-authoritative multiplayer experience where each player sees the same board state and action flow, including room lifecycle, seat claiming, and replay-friendly restarts.

Why It Exists

The system was designed to reduce reconnect pain and setup confusion so matches can resume quickly after refresh, temporary disconnects, or tunnel restarts.

Current Focus

Keep gameplay stable under live conditions, preserve fairness in hidden hand state, and ensure scoring behavior stays accurate while adding usability polish.

Open The Experience

Launch the app directly or use the stable room path for quick two-player sessions.
